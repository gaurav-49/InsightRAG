# Halcyon Robotics — Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

Policy owner: People Operations. Effective 1 September 2025.

## 1. Working Patterns

Halcyon operates a hybrid model. Office-based roles are expected in the office at least 2 days per week, and every team agrees one common anchor day on which the whole team is present. Manufacturing, laboratory and field service roles are site-based and are not eligible for hybrid working.

Fully remote working is available only for roles advertised as remote, or by exception approved by a vice president. Remote employees must live in a country where Halcyon has a legal entity: the United Kingdom, Germany, the United States or Japan.

## 2. Core Hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Meetings involving several time zones should rotate so the same people are not always inconvenienced.

## 3. Working from Abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days in any 12-month period, provided they get manager approval and complete the travel-work request form at least 2 weeks in advance. Working abroad for longer requires approval from People Operations and Legal because of tax and immigration rules. Access to Restricted data from outside the employee's home country is not allowed.

## 4. Home Office Equipment

New employees in hybrid or remote roles receive a one-off home office allowance of 600 dollars for a desk, chair or monitor. Remote employees also receive a monthly internet allowance of 40 dollars. Company laptops and peripherals remain Halcyon property and must be returned when employment ends.

## 5. Health and Safety

Employees working from home must complete the home workstation self-assessment within the first month. Halcyon will provide reasonable adjustments recommended by the assessment.

## 6. Security

Remote work must follow the Information Security Policy. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the company VPN connected. Confidential documents must not be printed at home.

## 7. Review

Managers may review an employee's hybrid arrangement if business needs change, with at least 4 weeks notice of any change to the agreed pattern.
