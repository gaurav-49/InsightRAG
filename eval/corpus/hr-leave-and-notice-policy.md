# Halcyon Robotics — Leave and Notice Policy

Policy owner: People Operations. Effective 1 February 2026. Applies to all employees of Halcyon Robotics Ltd and its subsidiaries. Contractors are governed by their individual statements of work and are not covered by this policy unless a section says otherwise.

## 1. Probation

Every new employee serves a probationary period of 90 calendar days starting on their first working day. During probation either party may end employment with 7 days written notice. A manager may extend probation once, by up to 45 days, if the extension is agreed in writing with People Operations before the original probation end date. Probation cannot be extended retroactively.

At the end of probation the manager completes a confirmation review. If no review is recorded within 10 working days of the probation end date, the employee is treated as confirmed.

## 2. Notice Periods

Once probation is complete, the notice period depends on role level:

- Individual contributors at levels L1 to L4 must give 30 days notice.
- Senior individual contributors and managers at levels L5 and L6 must give 60 days notice.
- Directors and above (L7+) must give 90 days notice.

The same notice periods apply when Halcyon ends employment, except in cases of gross misconduct. Notice must be given in writing to the employee's manager with a copy to People Operations; a message in a chat tool does not count as written notice.

Halcyon may, at its discretion, place an employee on garden leave for all or part of the notice period, or pay salary in lieu of notice. An employee may ask to shorten their notice period; the request is approved only if the manager and the People Operations partner both agree, and the agreement is recorded in the HR system.

## 3. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. After five years of continuous service the allowance rises to 28 days. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata allowance based on their contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited. Carry-over above 5 days requires written approval from a director and is granted only when leave was cancelled at the company's request.

Leave requests of 5 consecutive working days or more should be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance. Shorter requests should be submitted at least 3 working days in advance. Managers must respond to leave requests within 5 working days.

On leaving Halcyon, accrued but untaken leave is paid out at the employee's daily base rate. Leave taken in excess of the accrued amount is deducted from the final salary payment.

## 4. Sick Leave

Employees receive full pay for up to 15 days of sick leave in any rolling 12-month period. Absences of more than 3 consecutive calendar days require a medical certificate. Employees must inform their manager of sickness absence before 10:00 on the first day of absence, or as soon as reasonably practicable.

After 15 days in a rolling 12-month period, statutory sick pay applies. People Operations will arrange a return-to-work conversation after any absence longer than 10 working days.

## 5. Parental Leave

Birth parents are entitled to 26 weeks of parental leave at full pay, followed by up to 13 weeks at 50 percent pay. Non-birth parents, including adoptive parents and partners, are entitled to 12 weeks at full pay, which may be taken in one block or in up to three separate blocks within the first 12 months after birth or placement.

To be eligible for enhanced parental pay an employee must have completed 26 weeks of continuous service by the expected week of birth or placement. Employees with shorter service receive statutory pay only.

Employees returning from parental leave may request a phased return of up to 4 weeks at reduced hours on full pay.

## 6. Other Leave

- Bereavement leave: up to 10 days paid leave on the death of a close family member, and 3 days for other relatives.
- Jury service: full pay for the duration of jury service; employees must provide the court summons to People Operations.
- Volunteering: 2 paid volunteering days per calendar year for registered charities.
- Study leave: up to 5 days per year for exams related to an approved qualification.
- Sabbatical: employees with seven years of continuous service may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of up to 3 months.

## 7. Requesting and Recording Leave

All leave is requested and recorded in the Harbor HR system. Leave that is not recorded in Harbor is treated as unauthorised absence. Unauthorised absence of more than 2 consecutive working days is a disciplinary matter.

## 8. Questions

Questions about this policy should be sent to people-ops@halcyon.example. This policy is reviewed annually by People Operations and the Chief People Officer.
