# Halcyon Robotics Ltd — Minutes of the Board of Directors Meeting

Date: 11 March 2026. Location: London office and video conference. Chair: Eleanor Voss.

Present: Eleanor Voss (Chair), Marcus Lindqvist (CEO), Aiko Tanaka (CFO), Daniel Okafor (non-executive director), Sofia Marchetti (non-executive director). In attendance: Priya Raman (VP Product), Helen Carter (General Counsel, company secretary).

## 1. Minutes of the Previous Meeting

The minutes of the meeting held on 9 December 2025 were approved without amendment.

## 2. CEO Report

The CEO reported that fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was 18.4 million dollars, up 31 percent year on year, driven by ARM-X1 fleet expansions at two automotive customers. Full-year 2025 revenue was 61.2 million dollars. Customer churn for the year was 3 percent. The ARM-X2 launched to manufacturing in February 2026 with 140 units in the initial order backlog.

## 3. Financial Report

The CFO presented the 2026 budget. Planned revenue for 2026 is 84 million dollars. Gross margin target is 52 percent, up from 48 percent in 2025, mainly from lower component costs on the ARM-X2. Cash at the end of February 2026 was 42 million dollars, giving a runway of more than 30 months at the planned burn rate.

The board approved the 2026 budget.

## 4. Headcount Plan

The board approved a headcount plan to grow from 310 to 385 employees by the end of 2026. Of the 75 new roles, 40 are in engineering, 15 in manufacturing and field service, 12 in sales and 8 in general and administrative functions. Hiring in engineering is weighted towards firmware and safety engineering.

## 5. Manufacturing Expansion

Management proposed a second assembly line at the Coventry facility at a capital cost of 6.5 million dollars. The line would double ARM-X2 capacity to 400 units per month by the first quarter of 2027. After discussion the board approved the investment, subject to the CFO confirming equipment financing terms at no more than 7 percent interest.

## 6. Cloud Hosting Contract

The General Counsel reported on the Stratus Compute Services agreement. Availability in January 2026 was 99.87 percent, below the contracted level, and a service credit has been claimed. The board asked management to evaluate a second hosting provider before the contract's non-renewal notice deadline.

## 7. Risk and Compliance

The board reviewed the risk register. The top three risks are supply of harmonic drive gearboxes, a single-source dependency for the safety controller chip, and cybersecurity of connected fleets. Management will qualify a second gearbox supplier by the end of the third quarter of 2026. The ISO 27001 surveillance audit was passed with no major non-conformities.

## 8. Dividend

The board resolved not to declare a dividend for 2025 and to retain earnings for growth.

## 9. Any Other Business

Daniel Okafor announced his intention to step down as a non-executive director at the annual general meeting in June 2026. The nominations committee will begin a search for a replacement with manufacturing industry experience.

The next board meeting will be held on 10 June 2026. The meeting closed at 16:40.
