# Halcyon Robotics — Travel and Expense Policy

Policy owner: Finance. Version 4.1, effective 1 January 2026. This policy applies to employees and to contractors whose contract allows expense reimbursement.

## 1. Principles

Spend company money as if it were your own. Choose the lowest reasonable cost option that still lets you do the job safely and well. Expenses are reimbursed only when they are necessary for company business, supported by an itemised receipt, and submitted through the Ledgerly expense tool.

## 2. Booking Travel

All flights, rail and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal, TripDesk. Bookings made outside TripDesk are reimbursed only if they were at least 10 percent cheaper than the equivalent TripDesk fare, and a screenshot of the TripDesk comparison is attached to the claim.

International travel requires pre-approval from the traveller's director at least 14 days before departure. Domestic travel requires manager approval only.

### 2.1 Air Travel

Economy class is the default for all flights. Premium economy may be booked for flights with a scheduled duration of more than 6 hours. Business class may be booked only for flights with a scheduled duration of more than 10 hours and requires approval from a vice president. First class is never reimbursed.

Checked baggage fees are reimbursed for trips longer than 3 nights or when carrying company equipment. Seat selection fees are reimbursed up to 40 dollars per flight segment.

### 2.2 Rail and Ground Transport

Standard class rail is the default; first class rail is allowed for journeys longer than 3 hours. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed when public transport is impractical, when travelling between 22:00 and 06:00, or when carrying equipment. Personal car use is reimbursed at 0.45 dollars per mile, and mileage must be logged in Ledgerly with start and end locations.

Car rental is limited to intermediate size or smaller. Collision damage waiver cover should be declined because Halcyon's corporate policy already provides it.

### 2.3 Hotels

Nightly hotel rates, excluding taxes, may not exceed the following caps:

- Tier 1 cities (London, New York, San Francisco, Tokyo, Zurich): 300 dollars.
- Tier 2 cities (all other capital cities and Boston, Munich, Seattle): 220 dollars.
- All other locations: 160 dollars.

Stays that exceed the cap require director approval in TripDesk before booking.

## 3. Meals and Per Diem

When travelling overnight, meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to a daily limit of 75 dollars per day in Tier 1 cities and 60 dollars per day elsewhere. Alcohol is not reimbursable except as part of approved client entertainment. Meals on single-day trips are reimbursed only when the trip lasts more than 10 hours.

## 4. Client Entertainment

Client entertainment must be pre-approved by a director and is capped at 120 dollars per attendee, including tax and tip. The claim must list every attendee and their organisation. Gifts to clients may not exceed 50 dollars in value per recipient per year, and gifts to government officials are prohibited.

## 5. Submitting Claims

Expense claims must be submitted in Ledgerly within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed. Every expense above 25 dollars needs an itemised receipt; for lost receipts a signed missing-receipt declaration is accepted for a maximum of two items per quarter.

### 5.1 Approval Thresholds

- Claims up to 1,000 dollars are approved by the employee's manager.
- Claims above 1,000 dollars and up to 5,000 dollars also need director approval.
- Claims above 5,000 dollars also need approval from a vice president and a Finance business partner.

Approvers must act on a claim within 5 business days. Approved claims are paid in the next monthly payroll run.

## 6. Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year may request a corporate card. The corporate card may not be used for personal purchases, even if the employee intends to repay them. Card statements must be reconciled in Ledgerly by the 10th of the following month; two late reconciliations in a year lead to the card being suspended.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are never reimbursed: first class air travel, traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment such as in-room movies, loyalty programme fees, travel insurance purchased separately, and lost or stolen personal property.

## 8. Contact

Questions about this policy go to expenses@halcyon.example. Suspected misuse of company funds must be reported to the Finance Controller or through the confidential ethics line.
